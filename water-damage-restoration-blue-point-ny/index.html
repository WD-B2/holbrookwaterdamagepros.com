If water has entered a house in Blue Point, call the 24/7 emergency line from your phone, close the main water valve and keep out of any water that touched an outlet. The line serves all of ZIP 11715 from the Holbrook office, to the northwest.

A hamlet with a different town office

Blue Point is a hamlet of about 5,200 people and roughly 2,100 housing units. Its Wikipedia entry places it in the Town of Brookhaven, while neighboring Bayport is in the Town of Islip, even though the two share a school district. That boundary affects which building office reviews a repair, so call the town for your address before you plan a rebuild.

The Blue Point Avenue and Middle Road corridors hold the elementary school, the library and most daily traffic. A new library site on Middle Road, approved by voters in 2018, shows how the hamlet keeps reusing older buildings.

When water is spreading in Blue Point

Any water that moves, rises or keeps coming is an emergency. Blue Point crews arrive from the Holbrook office, and emergency dispatch is available around the clock for these streets, including at night and on holidays. The line stays on until you know what to do.

Insurance questions to ask early

In a bay-adjacent area, the first insurance question is whether the water came from inside the house or from outside. A burst pipe and rising yard water are handled very differently by most policies. Standard homeowners coverage usually excludes flood, and flood coverage is a separate policy. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

Take photos of the source, the water line on walls and every damaged item before anything moves. The flood damage cleanup page explains how water that came from outside is treated, and the structural drying page covers how drying is verified.

What to document

Source, water line, damaged items, serial numbers on electronics.

What to keep

Receipts for emergency purchases and any written scope you receive.

What not to do

Do not throw away damaged property before photos are taken.

Who to call

Your insurer, then the 24/7 line, in either order if water is still spreading.

What a wet Blue Point home needs first

Extraction comes first. Pumps and extraction units remove standing water, and wet vacuums finish the edges. The water extraction page describes the equipment and what to expect on arrival.

Contents matter too. Furniture, books, papers and electronics are separated into what can be dried and what cannot. Take photos of each item before it moves, and keep any item an insurer may want to see until it is cleared.

Standards and sources

Blue Point water damage questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Blue Point?

Blue Point uses ZIP 11715. The line covers the whole ZIP, including the Blue Point Avenue and Middle Road corridors. Give the address to confirm.

Is emergency water removal available in Blue Point?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. Same-day response is offered when available and no arrival time is promised. You will be told how to stay safe while help is arranged.

Which town's building department covers Blue Point?

Blue Point is listed in the Town of Brookhaven, though neighboring Bayport is in the Town of Islip. Confirm with the town for your own address.

Is flood damage covered by homeowners insurance?

Usually not. Flood coverage is a separate policy. A burst pipe inside the house is often covered. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

How soon should drying start?

As soon as the water is out. EPA guidance says to dry water-damaged material within 24 to 48 hours.
