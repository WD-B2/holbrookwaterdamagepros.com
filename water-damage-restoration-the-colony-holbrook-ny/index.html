Water Damage Restoration in The Colony, Holbrook, NY

The Colony is a gated townhouse community in Holbrook. Many listings say the association handles water, sewer and exteriors, so know who to call before you start.

Call the water damage line

A wet floor or a stained ceiling in a Colony townhouse raises two questions at once. How do I stop and dry it, and who is responsible for which part of the building? Stop the water if you can do it safely, photograph it and call the water damage line while you reach your management office.

What listings and local guides say about The Colony

Homes.com describes The Colony as a subdivision along Patchogue-Holbrook Road with private amenities such as pools and tennis courts. Resale listings describe a gated community with three inground pools, a clubhouse with a ballroom, courts, a gym and a playground. Townhouses there are listed with build years of 1990 and 1991 and with Colony Drive addresses, in the Sachem Central School District.

Boundaries and the number of homes were not confirmed, so this page does not state them. Listing descriptions also disagree on basements. One townhouse is listed with no basement and another with a fully finished one, so check your own unit.

Who handles what after a leak

Several resale listings say monthly association fees cover water, sewer, exterior maintenance, grounds and trash. That is a listing detail and may differ by building, so confirm it with your management office. In practice it suggests a water problem can sit between three parties: you, the association and the shared building systems. Your governing documents settle which is which.

Your unit's interior

Carpet, flooring, cabinets, fixtures and finishes are usually the first things to be wet and dried.

Shared building parts

Exterior walls, roofs and shared lines often fall to the association. Ask management and read your documents.

Neighboring units

A leak that crosses a shared wall affects two households. Tell the neighbor and keep the same photos for both.

Common areas

A problem in a clubhouse, pool building or hallway is reported to management, not handled by a resident.

With a basement and without one

In a unit with a finished basement, the lowest level holds water first and dries last, so the basement water damage page applies. In a unit with no basement, the living level is the lowest level. A kitchen supply line, a dishwasher hose or a washer drain then affects the floor, base cabinets and walls where you live, and the appliance leak page and the kitchen water damage page are the best places to start.

How regional storms fit, and how they do not

On August 18, 2024 the National Weather Service issued a flash flood warning naming Holbrook among the western Suffolk locations expected to flood, and the Governor later declared a disaster emergency for Suffolk County. These are county and regional records. None of the sources reviewed here reports damage inside The Colony, and its FEMA flood-zone status was not verified.

A short documentation list

  • Photograph the source and every wet surface before you move things.
  • Write down when you noticed the water and when you shut it off.
  • Keep the management office's response in writing.
  • Ask your insurer what your policy covers. Coverage depends on the cause and the policy terms.

Drying follows the same standard in any building, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed after the water is extracted. The water extraction page covers the first hour and the structural drying page explains how readings decide when it is done. A nearby community with a similar housing type has its own page, the Timber Ridge page. The main Holbrook water damage restoration page lists every service. To start now, call (934) 223-1875.

Standards and sources

  • ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published industry standard for professional water damage restoration. It defines the three water categories and the drying goals used on this page.
  • The EPA guide A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture, and Your Home advises drying water-damaged material within 24 to 48 hours.
  • FEMA's Flood Map Service Center lets you look up whether an address sits in a mapped flood zone.

The Colony water damage questions

Does the association fix water damage inside my townhouse?

That depends on your governing documents and the cause. Many associations handle shared and exterior parts, while interior finishes belong to the owner. Ask management, and keep your photos and any written reply.

Do all Colony townhouses have basements?

No. Listings show some units with finished basements and some with none, so confirm yours before you plan around one.

Who pays for drying after a burst pipe?

That depends on the source, the policy and the governing documents. Your insurer and your association can answer for your unit. The crew documents the work either way.

A leak started in a clubhouse or common area. Who handles it?

Report it to management in writing. Common areas are not handled by individual residents.

Can I call at night or on weekends?

The line is open around the clock. Same-day response is offered when available, and no arrival time is promised.
