Other places that call the line

People in Oakdale, Patchogue, Ronkonkoma, Farmingville, Centereach, Islandia, Sayville, Medford and Lake Grove also call the Holbrook line. These places do not have their own pages. Call (934) 223-1875, give the street address and the line will confirm coverage for that address. A second call to (934) 223-1875 after hours reaches the same line.

Which town handles permits

Holbrook is located mainly in the Town of Islip, and the section between Portion Road and the Long Island Rail Road tracks is in the Town of Brookhaven. Nearby hamlets split in a similar way, so give the street address when you ask a town office about a permit.

Pick the page closest to your problem

Every area is served by the same set of services. The full service list groups them by cause. Common starting points are water extraction for standing water, burst pipe water damage for failed lines and storm water damage for wind-driven rain.
