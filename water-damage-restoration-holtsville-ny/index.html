Which town's rules apply in Holtsville

Holtsville is a hamlet in Suffolk County, and it sits mainly in the Town of Brookhaven, with a southwestern portion in the Town of Islip, according to its Wikipedia entry. That split matters after a loss, because the building department that handles a repair permit depends on which side of the line your address falls. Ask the town office for your address before a rebuild starts.

About 18,900 people lived in Holtsville at the 2020 census, in roughly 6,600 housing units. That is a lot of kitchens, laundry rooms and water heaters in one place, and most water calls here begin inside the house.

When water is spreading in Holtsville

Treat it as an emergency when water is moving across a floor, dripping through a ceiling or rising from a drain. Every hour wet drywall and carpet stay wet raises the chance of mold, and EPA guidance says to dry water-damaged material within 24 to 48 hours. Dispatch for Holtsville is available around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Stop the source

Close the fixture shutoff or the main valve. Water heaters and washers have their own valves.

Power

Turn off breakers to wet rooms only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. Call the utility for any sparking.

Lift and move

Raise furniture legs onto foil or blocks and pull rugs and boxes out of the wet area.

Keep clear

Keep children and pets out of any water from a drain, a toilet or the outdoors.

Before we arrive: an emergency checklist for Holtsville homes

  1. Find the main shutoff and close it. Write down the time you did.
  2. Photograph the water, the source and any damaged belongings.
  3. Open cabinet doors under wet sinks and lift anything stored inside.
  4. Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see.
