A slab leak is a pipe leak you cannot see, under or inside the concrete floor. If you hear running water with every fixture off, find a warm spot on the floor, or watch your water meter turn when nothing is in use, close the main water valve and call the Holbrook 24/7 emergency line. The longer it runs, the more of the floor system it wets.

Warm spots on the floor, a running water meter or damp flooring with no source?

People usually search for signs of a slab leak before they know the name. A warm patch of floor, the sound of running water with every tap off or a meter that keeps turning all point to a pressurized line leaking under or in the concrete. The trade name for the cleanup is slab leak water damage restoration, and it is what the rest of this page covers.

Most Holbrook homes sit on a basement, a crawl space or a slab. Where there is a slab, the water lines can run through or under the concrete. A leak there rarely floods a room. It soaks the sub-slab soil, rises into flooring and walls and shows up as damp, warm or cracked spots.

Symptoms of a slab leak and what each one means

These are symptoms and not a diagnosis. A plumber confirms a slab leak with listening gear or a pressure test. The signs below are the reason to call a plumber and a restoration crew rather than wait.

Only a plumber can confirm a slab leak. Restoration handles what the water did to the building.

Hot water lines

A hot water leak under a slab can heat the floor above it. Do not stand on a floor that is hot or spongy. Close the main valve and keep children and pets off the area.

When a slab leak becomes an emergency

A slab leak is an emergency when the water is pressurized and the line has not been shut off. The main valve controls that. After it is closed, the immediate emergency is over for new water, but the water already in the building and under the floor keeps doing damage until it is dried.

How the cleanup differs from a surface leak

A slab leak does not leave a puddle. It leaves wet flooring, wet bases of walls and wet material above the slab. The crew checks the area with moisture meters and thermal imaging, removes flooring that cannot dry, and sets drying equipment. The plumber then locates and repairs the line, which sometimes means opening the slab.

Step 1

Shut off and confirm

The main is closed. A plumber tests and locates the leak.

Step 2

Map the moisture

Meters and thermal imaging show where the water traveled in floors and walls.

Step 3

Remove and dry

Flooring that cannot dry is removed. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the slab surface, wall bottoms and cavities.

Step 4

Repair and rebuild

After the plumber's repair, flooring, baseboard and wall finishes are replaced.

Drying a concrete slab takes longer than drying carpet, because concrete holds moisture deep inside. The structural drying page explains how readings are taken and how drying is verified. A leak that began in a supply line is related to a burst pipe, but the repair path is different because the line is buried.

Questions about this service

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Holbrook?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7. Same-day response is offered when available and no arrival time is promised. You are told how to shut off the main and keep people off a hot or wet floor.

How do I know if the damp spot on my floor is a slab leak?

A damp or warm spot with no visible source, plus a water meter that moves when every fixture is off, points toward a leak under the floor. Close the main valve and call. A plumber confirms it, and restoration dries what the water reached.

Does restoration find and fix the slab leak?

No. A licensed plumber locates and repairs the line. Restoration dries the building and rebuilds the floor and walls.

How do I check my meter for a leak?

Turn off every fixture and appliance that uses water, then watch the meter's small leak indicator. If it keeps moving, water is leaking somewhere after the meter.

Is a warm floor always a slab leak?

No. Radiant heat, sunlight and ducts can also warm a floor. A warm spot with a running meter is a stronger sign. A plumber confirms it.

How long does a slab take to dry?

Concrete holds moisture deep inside, so drying can take longer than carpet or drywall. The crew measures progress with meters and does not guess.
