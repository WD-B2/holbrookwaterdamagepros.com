Emergency Flood Damage Cleanup in Holbrook, NY

Rain, groundwater and surface water that entered from outside is treated as contaminated. The 24/7 line explains what to do first.

Flood damage cleanup starts with a safety decision, because water that came in from outside the house is not clean. If floodwater is inside your Holbrook home, keep people and pets out of it, stay away from outlets and the electrical panel, and call the 24/7 emergency line. Do not run a household vacuum or walk through the water barefoot.

This page covers water that entered from outside: heavy rain pooling against a foundation, groundwater coming up through a slab, or surface water crossing a yard and a threshold. Water from a broken pipe, a washer or a roof leak has a different source and a different page. The line between them matters because contaminated water changes what can be saved.

Flooding is an emergency for three reasons

First, outside water carries soil, fertilizer, sewage and runoff. Second, it usually covers a large area, so every hour adds more wet material. Third, power and fuel-fired equipment in a flooded lower level can be dangerous, so the first step is often making the space safe, not cleaning it.

Stay out until it is safe

  • Do not enter a flooded basement if water is near the electrical panel or the furnace.
  • Call 911 or the utility for a gas smell, sparking or downed wires before you call the restoration line.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.
  • Do not eat food or use medicine that touched floodwater.

What Holbrook's flooding history teaches

During the August 12 and 13, 2014 storm, the National Weather Service recorded 13.57 inches of rain at Islip, a New York State 24-hour record, and its event summary lists a 12.57-inch report from Holbrook. The same summary lists closures of the Long Island Expressway and Sunrise Highway at Bay Shore. For a homeowner, the lesson is that a rain event can overwhelm drainage faster than any sump pump or yard grading was sized for.

Whether your own address sits in a mapped flood zone is something you can check yourself on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center by entering the address. Ask the Town of Islip or the Town of Brookhaven building department about local rules before rebuilding, because Holbrook is split between the two towns.

How floodwater is categorized and handled

What the S500 standard means for a flooded house
MaterialTypical outcome after floodwaterWhy
Carpet and padRemoved and discardedPad holds contaminated water and cannot be cleaned reliably.
DrywallCut out at and above the wet lineWicking carries contamination up the wall.
Hardwood and laminateOften removedBoards swell and subfloor stays wet underneath.
Concrete and masonryCleaned and disinfected, then driedHard surfaces can be sanitized and dried in place.

Outcomes depend on the category of water and how long materials stayed wet. The crew explains each decision on site.

Standing water is pumped out first, then the structure is opened up, cleaned with disinfectants and dried. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard behind those steps, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it. Contents that touched floodwater are separated into what can be cleaned and what cannot.

After the water: drying and what comes next

Day 0

Make it safe

Utilities are checked, people are kept out and the extent of the water is documented with photographs.

Day 0 to 1

Pump and extract

Pumps remove standing water and extraction units remove what remains in floors and edges.

Day 1 to 2

Remove and clean

Wet porous materials are removed, surfaces are cleaned and disinfected.

Days 2 to 6

Dry and verify

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture meter readings reach the dry standard.

After

Rebuild

Drywall, flooring and trim are replaced under a written scope you approve.

Contaminated water and long contact with building materials raise mold risk. EPA guidance recommends drying within 24 to 48 hours, which is why work moves quickly after the water is out. If flooding came from a storm that also damaged the roof or siding, the storm water damage page covers the building-envelope side.

Flood insurance is a separate policy

Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood damage, and flood coverage is a separate policy. Ask your insurer which policy applies to your situation, and photograph everything before you move it. Do not assume what is or is not covered until you have read your policy.

What sets the price of flood cleanup
  • Water categoryFloodwater is contaminated, so protective gear, disposal and disinfecting add to scope.
  • Depth and durationDeeper and longer exposure moves water farther into walls and framing.
  • Square footage and levelsA flooded basement is a smaller job than flooded floors and a basement.
  • Materials removedCarpet, pad, drywall, insulation and flooring are removed and replaced rather than dried.
  • Drying and rebuildEquipment days and the rebuild are scoped separately after cleanup.

Questions about this service

Do you handle flood emergencies at night or on weekends in Holbrook?

Yes. The dispatch line is open around the clock, including during storms. Same-day response is offered when available and no arrival time is promised. You are told what to do safely while help is arranged.

Is floodwater always treated as contaminated?

Water that came from outside, from the ground or from a drain is generally treated as contaminated under the S500 standard. That drives the protective gear and the decision to remove porous materials.

Can flooded carpet be saved?

Usually not. Carpet pad holds contaminated water, and the carpet itself often cannot be cleaned to a safe standard. The crew explains the call before anything is removed.

Will homeowners insurance pay for flood damage?

Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood. A separate flood policy covers it. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and document everything with photographs.

How soon should drying start?

As soon as the water is out. EPA guidance says to dry water-damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, so equipment is set the same visit when possible.
