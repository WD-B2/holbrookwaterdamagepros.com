Ice split the pipe, and the thaw is releasing the water. Close the main valve and call the 24/7 line before the leak spreads.

Frozen pipe water damage often shows up when the weather warms, because the ice that sealed a split in the pipe melts and the water finally escapes. If you see water, hear running behind a wall, or notice a pipe that is frosted and bulging, close the main shutoff and call the Holbrook 24/7 emergency line. Do not use a flame or torch to thaw a pipe.

A frozen pipe with no leak is a plumbing problem. A frozen pipe that has split is a water damage problem, and the damage depends on how long the water ran before anyone noticed. Pipes in exterior walls, crawl spaces, garages and unheated basements are the usual places.

Thaw carefully and know when to stop

If a faucet gives only a trickle in freezing weather, a section of line may be frozen but not yet split. Open the faucet so water can flow as it thaws. Warm the area with a hair dryer or a space heater set a safe distance away and never leave a heater unattended.

What not to do

  • Never use an open flame, a propane torch or a heat gun on a pipe. A pipe can start a fire in the wall or the water can scald as it releases.
  • Do not leave a space heater running near wet walls or flooring.
  • If water is already visible, close the main valve before trying anything else.
  • If a ceiling is bulging, stay out from under it.

Why the damage is often found late

Ice acts like a plug. The pipe splits along its length, but the ice holds the water back until temperatures rise. That is why the first sign is often a wet ceiling or a damp baseboard on a mild afternoon after a cold night, not on the coldest morning.

Long Island has real winters, and the NWS Upton office publishes climate data for the area, so check weather.gov for freeze records near Islip rather than relying on a general claim. What matters for your house is where the pipes run. A line on an outside wall with thin insulation, a hose bib that was never drained, or a supply run through an unheated crawl space is the first place to look.

How restoration works after a freeze split

Step 1

Locate and isolate

Find the split, close the valve upstream and drain the line. A plumber repairs the pipe while the crew handles the water.

Step 2

Remove the standing water

Wet vacuums and extraction units pull water from floors, carpet and edges.

Step 3

Open and map

Moisture meters and thermal imaging show where water moved. Small openings in drywall let wet cavities dry.

Step 4

Dry and verify

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach the dry standard and are recorded for your files.

Thawed water that ran inside a wall cavity is the main reason moisture mapping matters here. Drywall can look dry on the surface while the insulation behind it stays wet for days. Insulation that has been soaked loses its value and usually has to be replaced, and the structural drying approach is described on the structural drying page.

Frozen pipe damage and insurance

Many homeowners policies treat a sudden freeze burst as a covered event, but some require that you kept the heat on or drained the system when the house was empty. Policies differ, so check yours or ask your insurer before you decide what to claim. Photographs of the frost, the split section and the wet materials help any claim.

  • Hours before discoveryA split caught the same hour is a small job and one that ran while you were away can wet several rooms.
  • Where the pipe runsA split in a wall cavity or ceiling often means openings and insulation replacement.

Questions about this service

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Holbrook?

Yes. The dispatch line answers around the clock, and pipe splits tend to show up at night or on weekends when heat is low. Same-day response is offered when available and no arrival time is promised.

The pipe is frozen but not leaking. Should I call?

A frozen pipe with no leak is best handled by a plumber. Open the faucet, warm the area safely and call a plumber if it does not clear. Call the restoration line if you see water or a wet ceiling.

Why did water show up after it warmed up?

Ice can plug a split pipe. When temperatures rise and the ice melts, the plug goes and the water flows through the crack. That is why many freeze breaks are found in a thaw.

Can wet insulation stay in the wall?

Usually not. Wet insulation holds water against framing and drywall and dries slowly. The crew opens the cavity, checks the readings and removes insulation that cannot be dried.

Is freeze damage a mold risk?

It can be if materials stay wet. EPA guidance says drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours prevents most mold growth, which is why drying equipment is set quickly.
