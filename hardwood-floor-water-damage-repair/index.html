Hardwood Floor Water Damage in Holbrook, NY

Wood and laminate floors swell when wet. Fast drying decides whether boards can be saved.

24/7 LINEHOLBROOK, NY
24/7 emergency line
(934) 223-1875Call Now

Hardwood floors react to water within hours. Boards absorb moisture, swell at the edges and cup, and the subfloor underneath holds water longer than the surface does. If wood or laminate flooring in your Holbrook home got wet, stop the source, lift rugs and furniture, and call the 24/7 line before you start sanding or refinishing.

This page covers solid hardwood, engineered wood and laminate. Each reacts differently. Solid wood can often be dried and refinished, engineered wood has less tolerance because of its layers, and laminate swells at the seams and rarely recovers.

Do not sand or refinish until a meter says the wood and subfloor are dry.

What not to do

  • Do not cover wet wood with a rug or plastic. It traps water.
  • Do not turn up the heat to dry it fast. Rapid drying can crack boards.
  • Do not sand or refinish until readings are dry.
  • Do not wait. Wood can start to cup within a day.

How wood floors are dried

Special mats and drying systems pull moisture out of the boards and the subfloor from the surface or from below. Air movers and dehumidifiers keep the room air dry. In some cases a few boards are removed to give water a path out. Drying is slow on purpose, because forcing it can split the wood.

Step 1

Extract and assess

Surface water is removed. Meters measure the boards and the subfloor.

Step 2

Set drying

Mats, air movers and dehumidifiers are placed over the wet area.

Step 3

Monitor

Readings are taken daily. Boards are watched for movement.

Step 4

Decide

When dry, boards are sanded and refinished or replaced. Not before.

Drying wood usually takes longer than drying carpet or drywall, often a week or more. The structural drying page explains how readings are taken. If the water came from an appliance, the appliance leak page covers the source. Wet floors over a crawl space are covered on the crawl space water damage page.

Refinish or replace

If boards dry flat and the finish holds, a sand and refinish often brings them back. If they stay cupped or warped, replacement is the honest answer. Matching old boards is difficult, so the scope may include a larger area for a uniform look. The decision is made on readings and flatness, not on guesses.

Questions about this service

Can a cupped hardwood floor be saved?

Often yes if it is dried slowly and fully. Many cupped floors flatten after the moisture is out. Severely buckled or blackened boards are usually replaced.

How soon do wood floors start to cup?

Within hours to a day, depending on how much water and how long it sat. Fast drying gives the best result.

Can I sand right after drying?

Not until readings confirm both boards and subfloor are dry. Sanding wet wood can lock in cupping and cause crowning.

Is laminate different?

Yes. Laminate has a fiberboard core that swells at the seams and rarely returns. It is often replaced.
