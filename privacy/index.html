Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

24/7 emergency line
(934) 223-1875Call Now

This policy explains what information Holbrook Water Damage Pros collects when you use this website or call our line, and how it is used. We keep it short because we collect little.

Information we receive

When you call (934) 223-1875, we may receive your phone number, your address and a description of the problem. If you email contact@holbrookwaterdamagepros.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. This site has no forms, so it does not collect information through them.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want to be recorded, tell the person who answers.

How we use information

  • To answer your call and arrange help at your address.
  • To improve the quality of the service.
  • To respond to your email.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Sharing

To arrange work at your address, we share the details needed with the restoration professionals who attend. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This site is a static site and sets no cookies of its own. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the map embeds load from Google Maps. Those services may collect technical data under their own policies.

Your choices and contact

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@holbrookwaterdamagepros.com or by calling (934) 223-1875. Mail can be sent to 5801 Sunrise Hwy, Holbrook, NY 11741.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
