How Much Does Mold Removal Cost in Holbrook, NY?

Price depends on size, location and what has to be rebuilt. Here is how quotes are built so you can compare them fairly.

By Holbrook Water Damage Pros · Published November 13, 2026 · General information, not legal or insurance advice.

Mold removal cost depends on how much area is affected, where the growth is, how much material has to come out and what it takes to contain the work. No honest company can quote a price without seeing the space, and any figure given over the phone is a guess. This guide explains what goes into a quote and what to look for when you compare more than one.

Search results often show wide price ranges because the jobs behind them differ so much. A patch on a bathroom ceiling and growth throughout a finished basement are not the same project. Instead of repeating numbers that cannot apply to your house, this page lists the cost drivers so you can see why two quotes differ.

Compare quotes line by line, not by the bottom number.

Why New York quotes come in pieces

New York's Mold Program, run by the State Department of Labor, requires licensed assessors and licensed remediators for projects over 10 contiguous square feet, and it keeps the two roles apart on the same project. So you often pay for an assessment and a protocol from one licensed company and for removal from another. That is not double billing, it is the structure the law sets up.

A clearance check after the work is another small line. It confirms the result before the rebuild covers the area. Ask who pays for it, and make sure it is done by someone independent of the remediator.

Quotes that should make you pause

  • A single price given over the phone with no inspection.
  • No mention of the water source or how it will be fixed.
  • No written scope, no containment plan and no state license shown.
  • A promise that mold will never return. Mold returns if moisture does.
  • A request for full payment up front.

A good quote names the area, the materials to be removed, the containment method, the clearance step and what is left for rebuild. It should also say what happens if hidden damage is found, such as extra wet drywall behind the wall you can see. Ask how change orders are handled and get any change in writing.

Insurance and mold

Mold coverage varies widely. Some policies cover mold that follows a covered water loss, some cap the amount and some exclude it. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and ask early, because drying quickly is the best way to avoid a mold claim at all. The structural drying page explains how drying is measured.

The mold remediation page describes the removal process step by step, including containment, removal and clearance, which helps you read a quote.

How to compare two mold quotes fairly

Line the quotes up side by side and mark what each includes. One may include containment and clearance while another leaves them out. One may include rebuild and another may stop at demolition. A lower bottom line can hide the gaps, which then show up as change orders.

Ask each company for the area it measured, the materials it plans to remove, the containment method, the schedule and the payment terms. Ask for its state license and insurance. Ask what happens if the work opens a wall and finds more than expected. The answers to these questions tell you more than the price.

Ask how the company decides the job is done. The right answer is a clearance check by an independent party, with a written report, before the rebuild begins.

Pricing notes for Holbrook homes

Holbrook is divided mainly between the Town of Islip and the Town of Brookhaven, and the building office for your address decides which approvals a rebuild needs. The Town of Islip's building division lists separate permit requirements for plumbing and HVAC work and electrical inspection certificates, so a mold job that exposes wiring or plumbing can add steps to the quote.

Homes in Holtsville and Bohemia face the same state mold rules as Holbrook, since the law is statewide. A neighbor's quote can still differ from yours, because the area, the location and the materials will not match.

Standards and sources

  • ANSI/IICRC S520 is the published standard for professional mold remediation.
  • The New York State Department of Labor's Mold Program licenses mold assessors and remediators for projects over 10 contiguous square feet.
  • The EPA guide A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture, and Your Home explains how moisture control prevents mold growth.

What to do first, and what not to do

  • Do not enter water that touches outlets, cords or a panel. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk first.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on standing water or run fans across suspected mold.
  • Do shut off the water source, lift valuables and photograph the damage.
  • Do keep children and pets out of water that came from a drain or outdoors.

Questions homeowners ask

Can you give a mold removal price over the phone?

No honest company can. The area, the location of growth and the materials all decide it. A written quote follows an inspection.

Why do two mold quotes differ so much?

They may scope different areas, include or leave out rebuild, or assume different containment. Compare line by line.

Is testing included in removal?

In New York, assessment and removal are kept apart on the same project, so they are usually separate lines from separate licensed companies.

Does homeowners insurance cover mold removal?

Sometimes, if the mold follows a covered water loss, and often with limits. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

Does removal include rebuilding the wall?

Usually not. Rebuild is a separate scope. Ask whether it is included and who does it.

Will the mold come back?

Mold returns when moisture does, and no company can promise that nothing will ever grow again. Fixing the source and drying the area are what lower the chance.
