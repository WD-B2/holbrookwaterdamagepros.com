Vacant and seasonal homes are a water risk

About one in six housing units in West Sayville was vacant at the 2020 census, a higher share than most nearby hamlets. The census does not say why, but empty and seasonal houses share one risk. A leak in a house no one visits can run for days before anyone sees it.

If you own a second home or are away often, a shutoff at the main and a neighbor with a key are cheap protection. When a leak is found late, expect more than a simple dry-out, because the water has had time to reach framing and floors. The structural drying page explains how that is measured.

A shutdown routine for a house you are away from

  • Close the main water valve before you leave for more than a few days.
  • Set heat to a safe minimum in cold months so lines do not freeze.
  • Ask a neighbor or relative to look inside after storms.
  • Know where the main valve is so someone can close it for you.

Winter is the other hazard for empty houses. A split line from a hard freeze is described on the frozen pipe water damage page. Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours in wet material, according to EPA guidance.
