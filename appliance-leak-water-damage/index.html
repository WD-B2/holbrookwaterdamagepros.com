An appliance leak is an emergency when water is still running or has already spread past the machine. Shut off the supply valve behind or beside the appliance, unplug it only if the cord and floor are dry, and call the Holbrook 24/7 emergency line. A failed washing machine hose can release hundreds of gallons in an hour.

This page covers the machines in a Holbrook home that use water: washers, dishwashers, refrigerators with ice makers, and the supply and drain lines that feed them. The cleanup is the same craft as any other water loss. What differs is where the water hides, because appliances sit against walls, over cabinets and on top of floors that hold water.

Appliance leak: what to do in the first five minutes

  1. Close the shutoff valve for the appliance. If you cannot find it, close the main water valve.
  2. Unplug the appliance only if the outlet and the floor in front of it are dry.
  3. Lift items off the floor and pull rugs and boxes out of the water.
  4. Photograph the appliance label, the hose and the wet area.
  5. Call the 24/7 line before you run a household vacuum or fans.

Water and a plugged-in machine

If water reaches the outlet or the cord, stay out and shut off the breaker from a dry spot. If you cannot reach the panel safely, call the utility or 911.

Replace rubber washer hoses with braided stainless lines and look at them yearly.

Water that comes from the supply side is clean when it starts, so the response is mostly extraction and drying. Water from a drain hose is different, because it carries detergent, lint and sometimes food waste. That can move it from clean to a category that needs cleaning before drying, and the crew decides on site.

Hidden water behind and under the machine

The visible puddle is not the loss. Water runs under cabinets, into wall cavities behind a washer and under flooring where it sits. A refrigerator ice maker line can drip for weeks behind the unit and never show until a floor softens or a smell appears. Moisture meters and thermal imaging find the water that hands and eyes cannot.

If the leak was on an upper floor, water often reaches the ceiling below, and the ceiling water damage page covers that side. In a kitchen the loss often travels into cabinetry, and the kitchen water damage page covers what happens to cabinets and counters.

How the cleanup goes

Step 1

Stop the water

The supply is closed and the appliance is pulled out so the wall and floor behind it can be reached.

Step 2

Extract

Wet vacuums and extraction units remove standing water from the floor and edges.

Step 3

Map and open

Meters check the cabinet base, wall bottoms and subfloor. Wet baseboards and toe-kicks are removed to let air in.

Step 4

Dry and verify

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach dry standards. Flooring is saved or replaced based on those readings.

A leaking dishwasher or washer that was caught the same day often means a day or two of drying. One that leaked for weeks often means removing flooring, cabinet bases and sometimes subfloor. The structural drying page explains how the readings are taken, and the water extraction page covers the first hour.

What sets the price of an appliance leak job
  • How long it leakedA short event dries in place. A slow leak often means replacement.
  • Floor typeTile dries differently from laminate, vinyl plank, hardwood or carpet.
  • Cabinets and wallsWet cabinet bases and drywall add removal and rebuild steps.
  • Water categoryA drain-hose release is treated differently from a clean supply line.
  • Floors affectedA leak on an upper floor adds a ceiling or wall below.

Questions about this service

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Holbrook?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7. Same-day response is offered when available and no arrival time is promised. You are told how to stop the water and stay safe while help is arranged.

Can a washing machine hose really release that much water?

Yes. A burst supply hose runs at full household pressure until someone closes the valve. That is why a yearly look at the hoses and a shutoff when you are away are worth the minute.

Is dishwasher water clean?

Supply water is clean. Drain-side water carries soap and food residue and is handled with more care. The crew confirms the category on site.

Do I need to pull the appliance out myself?

Not if you are unsure. Moving a heavy machine through water risks injury and a second leak. The crew moves it as part of the job.

Will insurance cover the appliance?

The appliance is usually a replacement cost you carry. Damage to the floor and walls from a sudden failure is often covered. Check your policy or ask your insurer.
