Wall and Drywall Water Damage in Holbrook, NY

Drywall soaks up water and holds it. The wall cavity behind it needs to be dried, not just the surface.

24/7 LINEHOLBROOK, NY
24/7 emergency line

Water in a wall is harder to see than water on a floor. Drywall wicks moisture upward, insulation holds it and the cavity stays wet long after the surface looks fine. If a wall in your Holbrook home is soft, stained, bubbling or smells musty, stop the source and call the 24/7 line for a moisture check.

Drywall is paper and gypsum. When wet, it softens and loses strength, and the paper becomes a food source for mold. Walls also hide wiring, plumbing and insulation, so water behind them is a bigger problem than the stain suggests.

Dry it or cut it out

Drywall that was wet for a short time with clean water can sometimes be dried in place. Drywall that sat wet, wicked up more than a couple of feet or touched contaminated water is usually cut out. A flood cut removes the wet lower section and leaves clean, dry board above. The cavity is then dried before new board goes in.

Insulation behind the wall is checked too. Fiberglass can hold water, and spray foam and cellulose react differently. Wet insulation that cannot dry is removed. Studs and plates can usually be saved if dried quickly.

Step 1

Map

Meters and thermal imaging show how far the water traveled.

Step 2

Open

Baseboards come off. Holes or flood cuts open the cavity so air can reach it.

Step 3

Dry

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cavity and studs. Readings are logged.

Step 4

Rebuild

New drywall, tape, texture and paint are installed once readings are dry.

Wall damage usually follows another loss. A burst pipe, an appliance leak or a storm can each put water in a wall. The structural drying page covers how cavities are dried.

Before you repaint

  • Wait until a meter reads dry.
  • Fix the source first.
  • Prime stains with a stain-blocking primer after drying.
  • If a stain returns, the leak is not fixed.

Dark spotting on drywall paper is a reason to read the mold remediation page. Wall cavities under a sink are discussed on the kitchen water damage page.

Questions about this service

Can I just replace the stained drywall?

Only if the source is fixed and the cavity is dry. Replacing board over wet framing traps water and brings the problem back.

What is a flood cut?

A cut made a set height above the floor to remove wet drywall and insulation below it. The cut is usually 2 feet or more above the wet line.

Will mold grow in a wet wall?

It can if material stays wet. EPA guidance says to dry water-damaged material within 24 to 48 hours.

How do I know the wall is dry?

A moisture meter reading compared to a dry wall in your home. Surface feel is not enough.
